Dense semantic caches supply a direct alternative to prototype summaries. At the CIFAR 95\% budget, the retained table gives 75.79 $\pm$ 2.50\% novel accuracy, compared with 73.08 $\pm$ 3.64\% for class-mean Tip-Adapter. On ImageNet-R, the corresponding values are 84.48 $\pm$ 0.72\% and 81.58 $\pm$ 1.72\%, with all three partitions feasible for both readouts. Across its five arrivals, retaining the classifier adds 1.48 $\pm$ 0.35 points in average incremental accuracy and 1.21 $\pm$ 0.46 points in final accuracy over this dense semantic-cache control.

We further evaluate 81 configurations spanning three datasets, three class partitions, one/two/five shots and three predeclared pseudo-split/support trials. Orders and trials are averaged within each class partition. At five shots, the paired average gain over recalibrated removal is 2.14 $\pm$ 0.37 points on ImageNet-R, 1.22 $\pm$ 1.18 on CIFAR and 0.78 $\pm$ 1.03 on Pets. The support-budget and readout rankings vary across operating conditions; Supplementary Section G gives every configuration family and both threshold policies. Across 648 append checks, fixed references preserve all restricted pre-offset candidate rankings, while dynamic references change rankings on each dataset. These checks isolate the reference mechanism separately from its accuracy trade-offs.
