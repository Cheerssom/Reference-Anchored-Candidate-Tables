We also reduce the base-validation queries used for threshold selection while keeping every fitted readout and fusion setting fixed. On ImageNet-R, the nominal 25\% budget uses 696--731 queries and gives 85.85 $\pm$ 0.38\% base and 84.98 $\pm$ 0.38\% novel test accuracy, compared with 86.01 $\pm$ 0.59\% and 84.73 $\pm$ 0.71\% using all 2,938--3,087 base queries.
