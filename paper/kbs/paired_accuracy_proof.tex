\subsection{Direct paired accuracy calibration}
Fix the models, arrivals and candidate thresholds independently of calibration.
Let independent class-stratified calibration pairs have correctness indicators
$R_i,C_i\in\{0,1\}$ and deterministic macro weights
$w_i=1/(B n_{c(i)})$, with $\sum_iw_i=1$ and $b=\max_iw_i$.
Define $A_X=\sum_iw_i\mathbb E X_i$ for $X\in\{C,R\}$.
For a class-macro target, each class's observations must follow its target
class-conditional law, or their averaged marginal law must equal that target.
Disjoint, deduplicated benchmark pools establish separation of data access;
independence and target-law correspondence remain explicit sampling assumptions.
Set $Z_i=C_i-rR_i\in[-r,1]$. Under the unsafe null
$\sum_iw_i\mathbb E Z_i\leq0$, for any fixed $0\leq\lambda<1/r$,
\begin{align}
 E_\lambda&=\prod_i[1+\lambda(w_i/b)Z_i]\geq0,\\
 \mathbb E E_\lambda
 &=\prod_i[1+\lambda(w_i/b)\mathbb E Z_i]
 \leq\exp\!\left(\frac{\lambda}{b}\sum_iw_i\mathbb E Z_i\right)
 \leq1.
\end{align}
Independence gives the equality; $1+x\leq e^x$ gives the first inequality.
Every factor is positive since $w_i/b\leq1$ and $\lambda r<1$.
For the prespecified grid
$\Lambda=\{.01,.025,.05,.1,.2,.3,.4,.5,.65,.8,.95,1\}$ at $r=.95$,
$E=|\Lambda|^{-1}\sum_{\lambda\in\Lambda}E_\lambda$ also has null expectation
at most one. Thus $p=\min(1,1/E)$ is a valid fixed-sample p-value by Markov's
inequality. Unlike the sufficient protection loss, $Z_i$ credits recovery
($R_i=0,C_i=1$) while charging destruction ($R_i=1,C_i=0$).

The test directly addresses $A_C\geq rA_R$ for the class-macro population.
It uses fixed sample sizes and mixture weights, with no optimization of
$\lambda$ or sample stopping based on the calibration observations.
Thresholds are tested from conservative to permissive; stop at the first
failure at level $\delta/5$. Rejecting any unsafe null entails rejecting the
first unsafe null in this prespecified sequence, of probability at most
$\delta/5$. A union bound covers five prespecified arrivals for each fixed
method/order/partition/policy under the class-conditional sampling model.
No independence between threshold tests or arrivals is required, and no
simultaneous guarantee across experimental configurations is asserted.
